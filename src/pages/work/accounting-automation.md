## The mandate

A Swiss operating company with roughly twenty employees, reporting under Swiss OR. Monthly close, quarterly VAT, annual accounts.

## Before

*Hours per month, error rate, how late the numbers were.*

## What I automated

- Bank and card transactions matched to invoices and categorised by rules, with anything unmatched routed to a review queue
- Recurring entries posted on schedule
- Intercompany and payroll accruals generated from source data
- Monthly reporting pack produced from the ledger in one step

## What stayed manual, and why

Reconciliation, the review of every exception, and the final sign-off. Automation moves the work from typing to checking; it doesn't remove the responsibility for the output. That's the part I'd never hand to a model.

## After

*Hours per month, what changed in error rate, how many days earlier the numbers were ready.*

## What this means for a founder

If your books are on a cloud ledger, most of the monthly close can be automated in a few weeks. What you're paying a finance person for afterwards is judgement, not data entry.
